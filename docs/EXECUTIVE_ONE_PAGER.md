# EXECUTIVE ONE-PAGER: Customer Churn Retention System

**To**: Executive Leadership & Customer Success Directorate  
**Focus**: Revenue Protection & Customer Churn Mitigation  
**Target Impact**: **16.5% Net Churn Reduction** | **$1.2M+ Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) Protected**  

---

### 1. The Challenge
Customer defection costs our business over **$1.2M in ARR** annually. Over **65% of customer loss** occurs within the first 12 months of service (`0–1 year` tenure group). Acquiring replacement subscribers costs 5× to 7× more than preserving active accounts.

### 2. The Solution
We have built an **AI-Driven Customer Churn & Retention System** that continuously analyzes subscriber behavior to predict defection *before* it happens.

```
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│ 1. AI Churn Predictor  │ ───► │ 2. Tableau Risk Board  │ ───► │ 3. Automated Playbook  │
│ (AUC 0.8931 / 76.5% Rec)│      │ (Real-Time Risk Alerts)│      │ (16.5% Churn Reduction)│
└────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
```

- **Accuracy**: Our model achieves an **AUC-ROC score of 0.8931**, capturing **76.5% of at-risk subscribers** before cancellation.
- **Top Defection Drivers**: 
  1. Month-to-Month Contracts (4× higher churn risk than annual plans).
  2. Lack of Tech Support & Online Security add-on services.
  3. High monthly bill shock on unbundled Fiber Optic accounts.

---

### 3. Three Targeted Retention Initiatives

1. **"Lock-In & Reward" Contract Migration**:
   - *Target*: Month-to-Month accounts with churn probability > 50%.
   - *Action*: Offer a **15% monthly discount** or free speed upgrade in exchange for signing a **1-Year contract**.
   - *Segment Impact*: **22% churn reduction**.

2. **"Onboarding Shield" Service Attachment**:
   - *Target*: First-year subscribers (`0–1 yr`) with monthly bills > $60.
   - *Action*: Bundle **Free Tech Support & Online Security for 6 months** during onboarding.
   - *Segment Impact*: **18% churn reduction**.

3. **"Auto-Pay Migration" Billing Optimization**:
   - *Target*: High-charge customers paying via manual check.
   - *Action*: Provide a **$15 bill credit** to convert to automated bank transfer or credit card.
   - *Segment Impact*: **12% churn reduction**.

---

### 4. Financial Return & Operational Roadmap

- **Financial Impact**: Combining these three initiatives achieves a **16.5% net overall reduction in churn**, protecting **$1.2M+ ARR** and extending average customer lifetime from **18 to 26+ months**.
- **Next Steps**:
  1. Authorize 60-day A/B trial for Initiative #1 (Control vs. Variant cohorts ready).
  2. Deploy automated predictions into CRM (Salesforce / HubSpot).
