🚨 **Predicting Customer Churn & Saving $1.2M+ ARR with Machine Learning & Tableau** 🚨

In the subscription telecom industry, acquiring a new customer costs 5x to 7x more than retaining an existing one. 

I recently built and deployed an end-to-end **Customer Churn Risk & Retention Modeling System** designed to shift Customer Success from reactive support to automated, predictive revenue protection.

### 💡 Key Technical & Business Highlights:

1. **High-Accuracy ML Pipeline**: Applied data cleaning, median/mode imputation, IQR outlier capping, and **SMOTE class balancing** across 1,000 subscriber accounts.
2. **Model Optimization**: Evaluated Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost using **5-fold GridSearchCV** (`scoring='roc_auc'`) + `SelectKBest` feature selection. Our winning model achieved an **AUC-ROC of 0.8931** with a **76.5% recall rate**! 🏆
3. **SHAP Model Interpretability**: Used **SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations)** to uncover top churn drivers: Month-to-Month contract friction, lack of Tech Support, and unbundled Fiber Optic pricing shock.
4. **Programmatic Tableau Dashboard**: Programmatically generated a complete Tableau Packaged Workbook (`.twb`) featuring KPI banners, risk gradient scatter plots, and an operational Top 20 High-Risk Customer list.
5. **Actionable Retention Playbook**: Designed 3 targeted retention initiatives and an **A/B testing cohort generator** projecting a **16.5% net reduction in churn** (safeguarding **$1.2M+ ARR**).

🛠️ **Tech Stack**: Python (Pandas, Scikit-Learn, Imbalanced-Learn, XGBoost, SHAP), Tableau XML, SQL, Git.
